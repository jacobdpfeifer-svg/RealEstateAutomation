# Texas buyer research — 2026-09-17

**The strongest initial discovery targets are small/regional principal buyers and local investment brokerages. No candidate has confirmed that it will buy this project's data.** This is a research conclusion to test with the owner after approval, not a sales forecast. Seller delivery remains blocked on the [contact-enrichment escalation](CONTACT_ENRICHMENT_REPAIR.md).

The [candidate dataset](../artifacts/buyer_research/tx_buyer_candidates.json) contains 21 entries across family/private buyers, regional operators, national buyers, wholesale brokerages, brand/franchise networks, iBuyers, and institutional firms. Eight are shortlisted for personal vetting. Five entries have independently verified brokerage identities/licenses, eleven are probable candidates, and five remain unverified. “Verified” applies to the stated evidence scope, **not** funding, every affiliate, a complete buy box, contact consent, or willingness to pay. Channels and competing intermediaries are deliberately retained and labeled; these are not 21 qualified end buyers.

## What the county evidence supports

**Harris County:** Big State expressly markets tax-lien problem purchases; TX Cash advertises Harris County and several alternative sale programs. These are more relevant starting hypotheses for unresolved seller problems than a general rental portfolio footprint. Houston House Buyers advertises distress capability but has an unresolved BBB identity/rating discrepancy and is on hold. Houston Deal Alert appears to distribute opportunities, so it belongs in a channel/competitor category until the actual purchasing entity is identified. None of these statements proves a recent closing or acceptance of a particular tax suit. [Big State](https://www.bigstatehomebuyers.com/cash-offers/hou/sell-house-cash), [TX Cash](https://www.txcashhomebuyers.com/), [Houston House Buyers](https://www.houstonhousebuyers.com/), [Houston Deal Alert](https://houstondealalert.com/).

Harris offers an official real-property instrument search and a separate bulk/FTP data-sales route. This supports a future record-based census, but no feed was purchased, requested or ingested here. Tax-sale material also introduces redemption and title questions: a suit, a judgment, and an auction purchase are different stages. The present seller records do not establish willingness to sell or an executable investment opportunity. [Harris Clerk search](https://www.cclerk.hctx.net/Applications/WebSearch/RP.aspx), [Harris Tax Office sales information](https://www.hctax.net/Property/TaxSales).

**Dallas County:** Four19 and Southern Hills expressly describe Dallas County coverage, making them stronger geographic evidence than a generic “DFW” label. New Western and NetWorth provide identifiable local brokerage operations; WhiteRock is a specialized SFR channel, rather than a confirmed principal buyer. DFW also includes counties outside this project: no Tarrant, Collin or Denton address should be silently admitted as Dallas County. [Four19 Dallas](https://www.four19properties.com/our-locations/dallas/), [Southern Hills](https://www.southernhillshomebuyers.com/), [New Western Dallas](https://www.newwestern.com/office/dallas-tx/), [NetWorth locations](https://networthrealtyusa.com/locations/), [WhiteRock](https://www.whiterocksfr.com/).

Dallas County links a public recording search, and the County Clerk publishes a bulk-data request route. These are separate from district-court suit ingestion and from the appraisal district's owner data. No deed extraction has yet established cash/repeat-buyer counts, transaction cadence, or demand by ZIP in either county. This report therefore makes no market-share, transaction-volume or price-band estimates. [Dallas recording](https://www.dallascounty.org/government/county-clerk/recording/), [county record-search directory](https://dallascounty.org/services/record-search/), [County Clerk request form](https://www.dallascounty.org/Assets/uploads/docs/county-clerk/County%20Clerk%20PIA%20Request%20Form.pdf), [DCAD data products](https://www.dallascad.org/DataProducts.aspx).

## Which categories could use a data/match product?

The fit assessments below are inferences from the cited sourcing models. No public intake form establishes authority to purchase data.

| Category | Existing sourcing evidence | Product hypothesis and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local/regional principal buyers | Direct seller forms and as-is acquisition marketing: Big State, Four19, Southern Hills and House Buyers of America | Best initial discovery tier for accurate ownership, fresh distress signals and filtered geography. Prove principal identity, cash capacity and incremental value; marketing alone does not establish these. |
| Wholesale/investment brokerages | New Western and NetWorth market off-market opportunities through local offices | Potential recurring research users, but also competitors/intermediaries. Their transaction/referral workflow cannot be adopted as permission to broker or assign property. |

## Legitimacy, complaints and misleading-channel risks

Seven relevant active TREC broker-company records were retrieved for five brands: WhiteRock; NetWorth Dallas/Fort Worth and Houston; New Western Dallas and Houston (United InvestexUSA entities); Offerpad Brokerage; and Opendoor Brokerage. Office disclosures support the mappings. Dates, exact legal names and source queries are preserved in [the license-check artifact](../artifacts/buyer_research/trec_license_checks.json). A registered office county is not the service territory, and a brokerage license does not certify an affiliated purchasing LLC or its funds. [TREC's data-set explanation](https://www.trec.texas.gov/public/high-value-data-sets), [official public records](https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Broker-and-Sales-Agent-License-Holder-Information/s7ft-44qi/about_data).

Amherst Capital Management LLC has an SEC adviser record, CRD 174130. That record is not blanket registration for the Amherst group, a particular fund or a property-holding company; the candidate-to-counterparty mapping remains unresolved. Praedia's fund/Reg D language remains self-asserted, with no matching registration established in this review. No inference of illegality follows merely from an unresolved registration or a principal buyer lacking a brokerage license. [SEC adviser record](https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/firm/summary/174130), [Praedia public site](https://home.praediacapital.com/).

- **Repricing/withdrawal complaints:** Southern Hills has a BBB complaint alleging a late $10,000 reduction. BBB's “answered” status is not a finding that a dispute was resolved. House Buyers of America has complaints about withdrawal and continued mailing, including incidents outside Texas. These are diligence leads, not adjudicated Texas misconduct. [Southern Hills complaints and responses](https://www.bbb.org/us/tx/plano/profile/real-estate-investing/southern-hills-home-buyers-0875-90908435/complaints), [House Buyers of America complaints and responses](https://www.bbb.org/us/va/vienna/profile/real-estate-investing/house-buyers-of-america-inc-0241-7002756/complaints).
- **Identity mismatch:** Houston House Buyers' site claims A+ BBB status; a same-name BBB listing is not rated/not accredited and has differing contact details. This could be a name collision. Hold the candidate until an exact entity/domain match resolves it; do not accuse either business of fraud. [Company](https://www.houstonhousebuyers.com/), [BBB listing](https://www.bbb.org/us/tx/houston/profile/real-estate-investing/houston-house-buyers-0915-90035780).
- **Actual regulatory action:** The FTC finalized a $62 million order against Opendoor concerning misleading seller-cost/savings claims. Separately, the FTC reports a $48 million court-entered settlement concerning Invitation Homes' renter practices. These are materially stronger evidence than anonymous reviews, but involve different conduct and should not be conflated. [Opendoor final order announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/10/ftc-approves-final-order-against-opendoor-labs-preventing-company-misleading-consumers-about-cost), [FTC FY2024 report](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/fy2024ftcagencyfinancialreport11152024.pdf).
- **Franchise/education or lead-selling diversion:** HomeVestors explicitly says corporate does not buy or sell houses; its franchise offering includes support/training. WeBuyHouses.com describes independently operated buyers and a brand-licensing business. These are documented channel distinctions, not findings of a scam. A paid franchise, mentoring or “buyer access” offer would be outside this task and evidence to reassess customer fit. [HomeVestors corporate statement](https://www.homevestors.com/contact/), [franchise FAQ](https://franchise.homevestors.com/about/franchise-faqs/), [WeBuyHouses.com terms](https://webuyhouses.com/terms).
- **Anonymous reviews and weakly corroborated entrants:** Reddit discussions about NetWorth/New Western include conflicting experiences; do not convert them into verified complaints. Provenial, Praedia, KMK and Houston Deal Alert remain unverified where independent entity/closing evidence is absent. Creative financing and a displayed client logo are not proof of cash or an active mandate. [Investor discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/realestateinvesting/comments/1k5otvq), [Provenial](https://provenial.com/), [Praedia](https://home.praediacapital.com/), [KMK](https://kmkinvestorgroup.com/), [Houston Deal Alert](https://houstondealalert.com/).

BBB accreditation and ratings are reputation signals, not legal registration or a closing guarantee. Targeted RealBiz searches did not produce usable entity-linked evidence. Review searches were selective, not a litigation/background-check clearance. No fraud verdict is assigned to any candidate in this research.

## Product/legal boundary requiring review

The proposed product sells research/matches. Calling a fee “data” does not decide its legal classification. Local wholesale brokerages, alternative listing programs and agent referral programs create the clearest scope questions: representation, property marketing without an interest, negotiation, assignment, closing-contingent compensation and introductions tied to a transaction must be reviewed before adopting any buyer's workflow. TREC's equitable-interest guidance and current rules are starting sources, not clearance for this business. No contract or assignment work is proposed. [TREC equitable-interest guidance](https://www.trec.texas.gov/article/sale-equitable-interests-real-estate-clarified), [TREC current rules](https://www.trec.texas.gov/agency-information/rules-and-laws/trec-rules), [Offerpad agent referral program](https://www.offerpad.com/agents/).

## Coverage limits and next decision

This is a broad, sourced screening across buyer types, not an exhaustive census of hidden holding LLCs or verified cash closings. Price limits, target ZIPs and cadence remain null when unsupported. Metro coverage is not county-wide parcel eligibility. No private investor's personal phone/email was scraped, no gated marketplace/MLS was accessed, and no signup, message, application or payment occurred.

The owner should review the eight candidates, resolve their flagged identity/role questions, and decide which category to test. Only a separately authorized discovery step can confirm the procurement contact, acceptable data, existing sources, coverage, freshness requirements and willingness to pay. Until then all records remain pending and outreach approval is false.
